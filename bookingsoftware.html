GETTING STARTED

General FAQs

Everything you need to know to get started.

✦ Can I use my own domain and branding?

Yes. Your site runs on your domain with your logo, colours and pages. The customer app and WhatsApp messages carry your name too.

◷ How long does it take to go live?

A landing page with online booking is usually ready within a few days of receiving your packages, prices and photos. Full websites with several pages take a little longer. You review everything on a private preview link first.

↗ I already have a website. Do I need to replace it?

No. Many customers keep their existing site and link “Book now” button to the booking page. You can move the rest over whenever you like.

⇄ Do you move my existing data?

Yes. Customers, products or packages, prices, staff and upcoming bookings come across from your current system or spreadsheet as part of setup. You send an export or give us access; we load it and you check it before going live.

✓ Is there a contract?

Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime. Annual plans are paid in advance and save two months.

₹
PLANS & PAYMENTS

Pricing FAQs

Clear answers about subscriptions and fees.

₹ When do I start paying?

When you are live and happy. We set up your site and bookings, move your data, and you check everything on a private link. The subscription starts on the day you go live, and it is monthly, so you can stop at any time.

✓ Can I start without choosing a plan?

Yes. We set up your site and bookings first. You choose a plan when you are ready to go live and take real bookings.

↗ What happens if I grow out of my plan?

Move up at any time. The difference is pro-rated. Nothing is lost or migrated: it is the same system with more switched on.

∞ Do you charge per booking or per order?

No. Unlimited bookings and orders on every plan.

▤ Do you take a commission or a transaction fee?

No. You pay the subscription and keep everything you sell. Card processing fees from Stripe, PayPal or your card terminal provider apply as usual.
